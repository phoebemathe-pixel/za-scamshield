# 🛡️ ZA-ScamShield

**AI protecting South Africans from financial scams - Detects ANY SA Bank Scam SMS**

> Final Project for GKS - Vanderbijlpark | October 2026

### 🎯 Accuracy: 85%+ | Detects 13+ SA Banks

Detects scams for:
**Capitec, FNB, ABSA, Standard Bank, Nedbank, TymeBank, Discovery, African Bank, Investec, Postbank, SARS, SASSA (R350), eWallet**

### ✨ What it does (V2 Features)
- 🔍 Detects which bank is being impersonated (FNB, Capitec, SARS, etc.)
- 🚨 Risk level: HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW RISK
- 📊 Scam Probability: 0-100% with progress bar
- 💡 Explains WHY it's a scam: +35 blocked, +25 link, +15 urgency
- 🇿🇦 Built for SA scams: R4,500 SARS refund, Capitec blocked, eWallet R350
